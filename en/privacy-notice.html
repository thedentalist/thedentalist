Purpose and legal basis of processing

The Controller processes health and identification data for the purposes set out in Section 4 of the Eüak., in particular to preserve and improve health, to provide treatment, to monitor the data subject’s state of health and to enforce patients’ rights. In further cases defined by law (for example, administrative or court proceedings) the Controller is entitled or obliged to process data.

Health data are special categories of personal data; they are processed under Article 9(2)(h) GDPR and the Eüak.

Details of each processing activity

Contact and appointment arrangement

  • Purpose: responding to enquiries received by phone or email, arranging appointments and keeping in touch. There is no online booking form.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at the data subject’s request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Data: name, phone number, email address, message content, requested treatment. Please do not send unnecessary health information or records by email.
  • Consequence of not providing data: without these data we cannot contact you or arrange an appointment.

Provision of healthcare

  • Purpose: dental care provided as outpatient specialist care, examinations, keeping the related records, fulfilling statutory reporting obligations and keeping in touch with the patient.
  • Legal basis: performance of the healthcare service contract and compliance with legal obligations (Article 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR); for health data, Article 9(2)(h) GDPR and the Eüak.
  • Data: identification and contact details, medical history, anamnesis, examination and treatment data, dental records and, where necessary, imaging and digital intraoral scans.
  • Consequence of not providing data: without these data we cannot provide healthcare.

Data processing and transfer via EESZT

  • Purpose: under Sections 35/A–35/N of the Eüak., the Controller must send the health documents and data listed in the Act, generated during care, to the Electronic Health Service Space (EESZT).
  • Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation, with regard to the data subject’s right of self-determination; under Section 35/H(1) of the Eüak., a patient may declare to what extent other healthcare providers may access their data in EESZT.
  • Data: identification data and health documentation.

Invoicing and retention of invoices

  • Purpose: issuing and retaining invoices for the services provided.
  • Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR; accounting and tax law).
  • Data: name, address and email of the patient and invoice recipient, and treatment-related details.

Complaint handling

  • Purpose: investigating complaints about healthcare services.
  • Legal basis: compliance with a legal obligation (Section 29 of the Eütv.).
  • Data: identification data of the complainant or patient and the personal data concerned by the complaint.

Dental laboratory work

  • Purpose: producing dental technical work needed for care.
  • Legal basis: performance of the healthcare service contract; for health data, Article 9(2)(h) GDPR.
  • Data: the patient’s name and the digital intraoral scan or impression needed for the work.

Website operation

  • Purpose: secure operation of the website. GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security; GitHub determines the retention period under its own privacy terms.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Gallery photographs

  • Purpose: presenting the clinic’s work.
  • Legal basis: the data subject’s signed written consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which may be withdrawn at any time via the privacy contact above.
  • Data: images of teeth and oral areas.

Retention

Health data (Section 30 of the Eüak.): health records must be retained for at least 30 years from data collection and the discharge report for at least 50 years. Diagnostic imaging must be retained for 10 years from its creation and the report for 30 years. After the mandatory period, data may continue to be kept for treatment or scientific research where justified.

Accounting documents (Section 169 of Act C of 2000 on Accounting): at least 8 years.

Appointment correspondence: until the matter is resolved, then only as long as necessary. For other data, the period set by the relevant law, or as long as processing rests on a legitimate interest.

Hungarian law in some cases requires mandatory data transfers (for example, in the epidemiological interest); in these cases, and where we receive an official decision, we comply in accordance with the rules on medical confidentiality.

Your rights

  • Right of access and information: you may request information on the data we hold about you, access and a copy. We provide information in writing, in plain language, as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest. It is free of charge unless you have already submitted a request for the same data this year, in which case a fee may be charged.
  • Rectification, erasure and restriction: you may request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data, erasure of your data or restriction of processing, under the conditions of the GDPR.
  • Right to object: on grounds relating to your particular situation, in the cases set out by law, you may object to the processing of your personal data at any time.
  • Data portability: where the legal conditions are met, you may request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of consent: where processing is based solely on your consent (for example, gallery photographs), you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.
  • Informing relatives and other persons: at registration or later, the patient may decide who may receive full or partial information about their illness, its likely outcome and changes in their condition, and who is excluded.
  • Access to health records: the patient (or legal representative) may receive information about their identification and health data and inspect their health records.

Some rights may be restricted by legal obligations applying to us as a healthcare provider: if the law requires us to retain your personal data, we cannot erase it.

To exercise your rights or ask a question, email thedentalist.hu@gmail.com or write to our registered office. After examining the matter, we will reply within 30 days at the latest. No automated decision-making or profiling takes place.

Data security

The Controller and, within its remit, any processor must ensure data security and take the technical and organisational measures necessary to enforce data-protection and confidentiality rules. Data must be protected in particular against unauthorised access, alteration, transmission, disclosure, erasure or destruction, and accidental destruction or damage. Everyone involved in processing must act with the greatest reasonable care to preserve the authenticity of the data and prevent unauthorised access.

If data are damaged: where health or personal data are damaged or destroyed, we attempt to restore them as far as possible from other available sources. Data in the electronic system are backed up; the third party is responsible for backing up data stored with it.

Complaints and judicial remedy

If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may:

  • contact the Controller directly using the details above; under Section 29 of the Eütv. you may complain about healthcare orally or in writing, and we will investigate and reply in writing within 30 working days at the latest; you may also turn to the patient rights representative to have a complaint investigated;

  • lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority, the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH): www.naih.hu, email: ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu.
